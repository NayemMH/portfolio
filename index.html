Flagship Publication · Peer-Reviewed · Nature Portfolio
Divide and Recombine Approaches for Fitting Logistic Regression to Large-Scale Health Surveillance Data: Application to Diabetes Risk Prediction in BRFSS
Nayem, M.M.H., Biswas, S.C. (2026)
Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio) · Vol. 16, Article 15980 · DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-46927-7

Applies divide-and-recombine (D&R) statistical methodology to fit logistic regression models on the BRFSS dataset — one of the world's largest health surveillance systems. Demonstrates that D&R estimates recover full-data inferential accuracy with dramatically reduced computational requirements, enabling scalable diabetes risk prediction at population scale.

Scalable Poisson Regression via Divide and Recombine with Asymptotic Theory, Monte Carlo Simulation, and Application to Cardiovascular Risk Factor Burden in BRFSS
Nayem, M.M.H., Biswas, S.C. (2026)
Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio) · 2026 · DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-64788-y

Extends the divide-and-recombine framework to Poisson regression for large-scale count data. Establishes the asymptotic properties of the recombined estimator, validates the theory through Monte Carlo simulation, and applies the method to cardiovascular risk factor burden in BRFSS — showing that partitioned estimation matches full-data inference at a fraction of the memory and compute cost.

drglm: Fitting Linear and Generalized Linear Models in a Divide and Recombine Approach to Large Data Sets
Nayem, M. (2024) · R package v1.0 · Author & Maintainer
CRAN R Package · Published July 31, 2024 · DOI: 10.32614/CRAN.package.drglm

An open-source R package enabling memory-efficient fitting of linear and generalized linear models — including multinomial logistic regression — on datasets too large to fit in RAM. Uses the divide-and-recombine statistical framework to produce statistically valid estimates equivalent to full-data analysis.

Application of Generalized Linear Models in Big Data: A “Divide and Recombine” Approach in R
Nayem, M.M.H. · Supervisor: Biswas, S.C.
Department of Statistics, University of Chittagong · M.Sc. in Statistics

The methodological foundation for the divide-and-recombine research programme: a theoretical and empirical treatment of fitting GLMs — linear, logistic, Poisson, and multinomial — to datasets that exceed available memory, implemented end-to-end in R. drglm — Fitting Linear and Generalized Linear Models in a “Divide and Recombine” Approach to Large Data Sets
Author & Maintainer
R Package v1.0 · CRAN · Published 31 July 2024 · DOI: 10.32614/CRAN.package.drglm

An open-source R package that brings the divide-and-recombine framework to everyday modelling work. It fits linear, generalized linear and multinomial logistic regression models to data sets far larger than available memory by partitioning the data, estimating within each partition, and recombining the sub-estimates into statistically valid full-data equivalents — with a formula interface familiar to any glm() user.
